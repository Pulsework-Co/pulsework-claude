# Pulsework for Claude

Pulsework writes your morning and your evening from your real day.
Your Claude reads your Slack, mail, calendar and the folders you choose, and hands Pulsework a short digest with quotes.
Pulsework writes the morning and the evening from it, and you read and answer them here, in Claude.

## Add it and connect

1. In Claude, open Customize, then Plugins, then Add marketplace, and enter `Pulsework-Co/pulsework-claude`.
2. Add the Pulsework plugin, open its Connectors tab and press Connect. If you have no Pulsework account, this step makes one.
3. Say "set up Pulsework". Your Claude asks a few questions, one at a time, and sets up your daily runs.

More about Pulsework: https://pulsework.co

## What leaves your Claude

- The short digest at open and close from Slack, mail, calendar and the folders you choose: quoted lines with who, where and when, who appeared with counts, and what was read.
- The settings you give it in setup: goals (including pasted team and company text), this week's lines, who you update, meetings, your morning and evening times and time zone, and where to look (including folder paths).
- Notes and answers you give it.
- If you leave the switch on in Pulsework Settings, a few lines about work you did with it.

## What never leaves

- Your conversations.
- Your Claude memory.
- Files outside the folders you chose, and anything beyond the quoted lines Pulsework asked for from those folders.
- Anything you did not ask it to send.

Pulsework never sends a message, a mail or a reply on your behalf.

## How to stop

Disconnect in Pulsework Settings under Your AI tool, press Disconnect on the plugin's Connectors tab in Claude, or remove the plugin. Any one of these stops everything above.
